# Cadenza — 90-second demo script

Screen recording of the real thing, 1440×900, browser at http://127.0.0.1:8010, voice-over in English, captions.

| Time | Picture | Voice-over |
|---|---|---|
| 0:00–0:08 | Blank canvas, the Cadenza bar at the bottom. | “This is a Symfony app. Its owner isn't a developer — she just talks to it.” |
| 0:08–0:25 | Type *“How could the website of my yoga studio look?”*. The trace streams; three live thumbnails appear (warm, elegant, minimal). Click *Choose this one*. | “Symfony AI writes the content once and offers three design directions — real pages, rendered live.” |
| 0:25–0:35 | The sketch fills the page; hover shows *new* outlines; click *Approve*; View Transition to the published site; toast with *Undo*. | “Nothing goes live without approval. Every version can be restored.” |
| 0:35–0:50 | Attach `glina-cennik.xlsx`: *“Make a shop from my price list, with filters.”* Trace: reading the spreadsheet → importing 12 records → sketch with a filtered product grid; click a chip, open a product page. | “Her spreadsheet becomes a catalogue — typed columns, filters, a page per product.” |
| 0:50–1:05 | Phone icon → QR code. Phone camera scans it, picks photos; thumbnails arrive live in the panel; albums *“Gdańsk, lipiec 2024”*; the agent builds a gallery. | “Photos straight from her phone — no account, no app. EXIF dates and places become albums.” |
| 1:05–1:15 | Pencil: draw three boxes with labels; *Add to message*; the sketch follows the drawing. | “She can even draw what she means.” |
| 1:15–1:25 | Crosshair on a heading → *“shorter, warmer”*; the changed node pulses in the sketch. | “Point at anything and say what to change.” |
| 1:25–1:30 | Diagram: Agent ↔ MCP (agent / control servers) ↔ Symfony app; logos Symfony AI, MCP SDK, Mate, Messenger, Workflow, UX Toolkit. | “Symfony AI, the PHP MCP SDK and the whole Symfony toolbox. Cadenza — your Symfony app improvises.” |

Before recording: `./cadenza reset`, a warm model (first answers are slower), phone on the same Wi-Fi.
